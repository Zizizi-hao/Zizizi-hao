## 📌 About Me

I am a **Ph.D. student at Southeast University and SLAI**, focusing on **end-to-end autonomous driving and embodied AI**. My research interests include **representation learning**, **world models**, and **reinforcement learning**. 

My recent work includes:

- **[ViRA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09695)**: aligning visual representations to improve end-to-end autonomous driving.
- **[NegWAM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08111)**: learning safety boundaries from near-expert failure trajectories for world-action models.

I received my **master's degree from Hunan University**, where I was supervised by **Prof. Chenhui Liu**. My research also explores **traffic agent behavior**.

For more about my background, projects, and publications, visit my [personal website](https://zizizi-hao.github.io/) or see my [CV](./zzh_cv_20261112.pdf).
